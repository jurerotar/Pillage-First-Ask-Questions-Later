## Your first village

Your village is the centre of your run. It produces resources, houses your population, trains troops and unlocks most of the decisions you make every session.

A village has resource fields around it and building slots inside it. Resource fields improve production. Buildings unlock actions, storage, training, research, celebrations, expansion and defensive options.

## Expansion

One village can become strong, but a network of villages is stronger. Expansion lets you specialise: one village can feed troops, another can push infrastructure, and another can sit close to targets you care about.
