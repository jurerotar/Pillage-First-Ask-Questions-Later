## The four resources

Wood, clay, iron and crop drive almost everything. Buildings, research and troops all ask for a mix of resources, so a good village is not only about producing more; it is about producing the right thing for the next bottleneck.

## Storage and crop

Warehouses and granaries decide how much you can hold. Crop is also tied to upkeep, so population and troops can make your economy feel tight even when the other resources look healthy.
