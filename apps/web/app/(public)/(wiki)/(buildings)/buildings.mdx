## Building slots

Buildings turn a village from a resource pile into a working base. Some buildings are economic, some military, and some exist to unlock specific systems like research, trade, demolition or expansion.

Select an empty building slot to choose what to construct. Select an occupied slot to open the building details and review the next level’s costs, build time, and requirements.

## Upgrade faster

On large screens, hover over a building to reveal the upgrade control when an upgrade is available.

<WikiCallout kind="tip" title="Upgrade with a long press">
  On touch layouts, press and hold an occupied building for one second to start the next upgrade. A tap opens the building details.
</WikiCallout>

## Queues

Construction takes time, and the queue is one of the main pacing systems. The Main Building improves construction speed and gives you better control over the village as it grows.

The construction queue shows active upgrades with a countdown, followed by scheduled upgrades marked **In queue**.

## Scheduled construction

Scheduled construction is created when you start a building level on a field that already has construction queued. The current level stays active, and the extra level waits behind it as an In queue entry.

Schedule several levels on the same field to keep building after the current timer finishes. You can cancel scheduled entries or reorder them by dragging their handles in the queue.

<WikiCallout kind="note" title="Keep levels in order">
  The queue rejects any order that would build levels for the same field out of sequence.
</WikiCallout>
