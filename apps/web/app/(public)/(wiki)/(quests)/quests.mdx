## Why quests exist

Quests give structure without forcing one exact build order. They point you toward important systems and reward you for making steady progress.

## Collecting rewards

Quest rewards are often best used to push through the next bottleneck.

<WikiCallout kind="warning" title="Check storage before collecting">
  Any part of a resource reward that exceeds your available warehouse
  or granary space is lost. Check your capacity before selecting{' '}
  <strong>Collect reward</strong>.
</WikiCallout>
